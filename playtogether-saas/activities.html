Activity library

Ready-to-use inclusive play cards

Each card includes steps, inclusion tips, safeguarding notes and adult language.

Ages 6–10

The Choice Jar

Children pick a simple play option from a jar. They can say “pass” without punishment.

  • Use picture cards.
  • Offer quiet roles.
  • Rotate choice fairly.
Ages 5–9

Build Together

A cooperative block-building game where every child has a role: designer, builder, helper or storyteller.

  • Assign non-speaking roles.
  • Celebrate process.
  • Avoid speed pressure.
Ages 7–12

Emotion Map

Children draw zones for feelings, safe spaces and helpful adults before a group activity starts.

  • Use colours, not labels only.
  • No child forced to share.
  • Adults model first.
Ages 8–12

Kindness Circle

A calm circle game where children pass a soft object and choose a compliment, question or pass card.

  • Permit silence.
  • Use opt-in sharing.
  • Stop teasing early.
Ages 6–11

Movement Mission

A sensory-friendly movement activity with stations for stretching, balancing, breathing and teamwork.

  • No elimination rounds.
  • Offer rest station.
  • Use visual timer.
Adult guide

De-escalation Script

A practical adult card for reading distress and calming conflict without blame or public shame.

  • Name the need.
  • Lower voice.
  • Offer two safe choices.

Find best activity

For high-energy groups choose Movement Mission. For anxiety, language barriers or conflict risk choose The Choice Jar or Emotion Map.

The Choice Jar

Steps: prepare 8 visual choices, invite each child to pick, allow pass, then reflect calmly. Safeguarding: repeated refusal may mean distress, not defiance.

Build Together

Steps: agree a theme, choose roles, build for 10 minutes, let the storyteller explain. Tip: give non-verbal children designer/helper roles.

Emotion Map

Steps: draw calm, worried and help zones. Children add symbols privately. Safeguarding: watch for signs of fear, freezing or isolation.

Kindness Circle

Steps: pass a soft object, pick compliment/question/pass, close with one group rule. Adult script: “We do not laugh at someone’s answer.”

Movement Mission

Steps: set 4 stations, rotate slowly, allow rest, end with breathing. Inclusion: no winners, no elimination, no public correction.

De-escalation Script

Use: “I can see this is hard. You are safe. You can choose quiet helper or take two minutes with me.” Avoid: “Stop being difficult.”
