Mission

A safer, kinder way to plan children’s play.

PlayTogether Hub supports parents, teachers and caregivers with practical, inclusive and trauma-aware play guidance.

Why this platform exists

The project proposal focuses on inclusive play for children from diverse backgrounds, including neurodiverse children and children from immigrant communities. It also asks for simple guidance for parents, teachers and caregivers, with activity cards, safeguarding notes and readable design.

This SaaS version turns that into a product: adults can select a child profile, receive matched activities, and share simple play plans.

Safeguarding note

This educational prototype does not replace professional safeguarding advice. It helps adults notice distress, avoid public shame, reduce conflict triggers and escalate concerns to the right safeguarding lead when needed.

Parent and teacher tips

Simple language that changes the atmosphere

Replace judgement

Say: “I can see this feels difficult.” Not: “You are being naughty.”

Offer two choices

“You can be the helper or take a quiet pause.” Choice reduces power struggles.

Protect dignity

Correct privately, praise publicly, and never force a child to perform emotions.
